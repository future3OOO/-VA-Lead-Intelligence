# ADR-007 — Scoring and taxonomy as configuration

**Status:** accepted<br />
**Date:** 2026-07-15

## Context

Sales and product teams need to tune lead qualification without deploying code.

## Decision

Capability taxonomy and scorecards are YAML under `config/capability-taxonomy/` and `config/scorecards/`. The scorer loads them at startup and validates with JSON Schema.

## Consequences

Scorecard changes are versioned, auditable, and testable against the frozen benchmark before production.
